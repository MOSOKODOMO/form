# Catalogue source audit, 10 October 2026

21 public Shop listings were compared with their stored primary supplier links using the visible supplier pages. 19 source pages currently contain real product listings; the Decormate and Benme pages say the information is unavailable. This is evidence of listings, not a guarantee the supplier is legitimate, stock exists or delivered quality matches its claims.

18 catalogue renders were generated with built-in image_gen and visually compared to supplier images. They are labelled AI RENDER in the shop and on product pages, with variant/pack/set limitations beside the gallery. Supplier photos were used as references only, remain in ignored local drafts, and were not republished. Existing photo-permission fields remain unchanged.

The exact Yuming 60 cm towel bar could not be confidently matched to its mixed accessory photos. Yuming, Decormate and Benme retain their placeholders and confirmation notes. No guessed product images were published for these three.

## Information that needed correction or qualification

- All 21 records have zero recorded FI physical product proof and FI buyer review points. Existing supplier store ratings must not be presented as FI buyer reviews or proof of this particular item. General “verified products/makers” wording in the Shop and product pages was replaced with sourced-product language.
- Company audit badges, claimed registrations, supplier longevity, staff counts, certifications and performance figures on supplier marketplaces are source claims. Independent issuer/register checks were not completed in this audit. The research scores were not increased because of the renders.
- Unsupported promotional origin stories were replaced by the observed source evidence and its limits. Previous savings comparisons were benchmarks against other retailers, not evidence these are identical products. Those comparisons were not substantiated by this audit.
- Xinjun's bamboo photo shows a two-post cabinet pull; its public name was corrected from T-bar to cabinet pull.
- Landonbath's accessory attributes say stainless steel; generic description text also mentions brass/faucets. Its brass-only product name and material were corrected and flagged.
- Contop 2007BG has conflicting brass/stainless material details. Landonbath and Jiangshuntong have unclear set contents. These records require confirmation and cannot lead directly to checkout, even if checkout links are added later.
- Yuming's mixed listing does not establish the exact named model/photo match. It also requires confirmation before checkout.
- The two unavailable links are not evidence the products were invented; their present identity and availability remain unverified and checkout is blocked until rechecked.
- The first three existing Stripe links are test links. This audit did not activate live payments.
- FI's warehouse inspection, free-delivery promises, landed prices, 10% margin, taxes, freight and stock were not independently validated by supplier-page existence. Existing AUD prices were preserved. A future supplier quote and complete landed-cost calculation are needed to substantiate final selling prices.
- Inspection reduces defects; source-page existence cannot support a 100% defect-free guarantee.
